## 1. Executive Financial Summary

NVIDIA Corporation reported record financial results for its fourth quarter and fiscal year 2025. Driven by the global expansion of accelerated computing and generative artificial intelligence, the company achieved unprecedented operational scale across all key financial indicators.

### Full Fiscal Year 2025 Highlights:
- **Total Revenue:** $96,310 million ($96.31 billion), an increase of 122% compared to $43,482 million in fiscal year 2024.
- **GAAP Gross Margin:** 75.0%, up 230 basis points from 72.7% in the prior fiscal year.
- **Non-GAAP Gross Margin:** 75.8%, up 200 basis points from 73.8% in fiscal year 2024.
- **GAAP Operating Income:** $61,240 million ($61.24 billion), an increase of 86% year-over-year.
- **Non-GAAP Operating Income:** $63,810 million ($63.81 billion), up 88% year-over-year.
- **GAAP Net Income:** $53,220 million ($53.22 billion), delivering diluted earnings per share (EPS) of $2.14, compared to $1.19 in fiscal year 2024 (reflecting the 10-for-1 stock split).
- **Non-GAAP Net Income:** $55,450 million ($55.45 billion), with non-GAAP diluted EPS of $2.23.
- **Operating Cash Flow:** $41,830 million ($41.83 billion), compared to $28,090 million in fiscal year 2024.
- **Free Cash Flow:** $38,410 million ($38.41 billion).

### Fourth Quarter Fiscal 2025 Highlights:
- **Quarterly Revenue:** $37,520 million ($37.52 billion), up 94% from $19,309 million in Q4 FY24, and up 7% sequentially from Q3 FY25 ($35,082 million).
- **Q4 GAAP Gross Margin:** 73.5%, compared to 76.0% in Q4 FY24.
- **Q4 Non-GAAP Gross Margin:** 74.2%, compared to 76.7% in Q4 FY24.
- **Q4 GAAP Operating Income:** $23,450 million ($23.45 billion).
- **Q4 GAAP Net Income:** $20,110 million ($20.11 billion), with diluted EPS of $0.81.
- **Q4 Non-GAAP Net Income:** $20,980 million ($20.98 billion), with non-GAAP diluted EPS of $0.85.

### Detailed Analysis of Business Domains:

#### A. Data Center Platform ($87.82B, 91.2% of Total FY25 Revenue)
The Data Center segment represents the foundational engine of NVIDIA's revenue growth. Compute revenue grew 154% to $76.2 billion, driven by the massive deployment of the Hopper architecture (H100 and H200 Tensor Core GPUs) by cloud service providers, consumer internet companies, and enterprise generative AI developers. Initial production shipments of the Blackwell architecture began in Q4 FY25. 

Networking revenue inside Data Center surged to $11.62 billion for the full year and $3.20 billion in Q4, up 134% YoY. This surge was catalyzed by rapid adoption of Quantum-2 InfiniBand systems and the NVIDIA Spectrum-X end-to-end Ethernet networking platform purpose-built for generative AI multi-tenant clusters.

#### B. Gaming and AI PC Platform ($11.45B, 11.9% of Total FY25 Revenue)
Gaming revenue expanded to $11.45 billion, supported by sustained retail demand for GeForce RTX 40 Series GPUs, particularly the RTX 4070, 4080, and 4090 Super editions. NVIDIA noted accelerating demand for AI PCs equipped with RTX Tensor Cores, capable of executing generative AI workloads locally with up to 1,300 TOPS of AI processing capability. Over 100 million GeForce RTX GPUs are now in active consumer use worldwide.

#### C. Professional Visualization Platform ($1.84B, 1.9% of Total FY25 Revenue)
Professional Visualization demonstrated renewed momentum driven by enterprise migration to RTX Ada generation desktop workstations. Major customer adoption occurred across automotive design, healthcare imaging, and architecture, with industrial digital twin workflows executing inside NVIDIA Omniverse Enterprise.

#### D. Automotive and Robotics Platform ($1.71B, 1.8% of Total FY25 Revenue)
Automotive achieved a record run-rate, surpassing $500 million in quarterly revenue for the first time in Q4 ($512 million). The primary growth catalyst was autonomous vehicle computing driven by NVIDIA DRIVE Orin system-on-a-chip (SoC) adoption across global electric vehicle original equipment manufacturers (OEMs), alongside new tier-1 automotive supplier design wins for the next-generation centralized DRIVE Thor computer.

## 3. Operating Expenses, Profitability, and Balance Sheet Health

### Operating Expenses:
- **Research & Development (R&D):** FY25 R&D investments reached $12,980 million ($12.98 billion), representing 13.5% of total revenue. Investments concentrated on next-generation computing architectures (Blackwell and Rubin), advanced silicon packaging, cuDNN/TensorRT software optimizations, and physical robotics software.
- **Sales, General & Administrative (SG&A):** Totaled $3,920 million ($3.92 billion), or 4.1% of revenue.
- **Total Operating Expenses:** $16,900 million ($16.90 billion), an increase of 49% YoY, demonstrating high operating leverage against a 122% revenue increase.

### Balance Sheet & Liquidity:
- **Cash, Cash Equivalents & Marketable Securities:** NVIDIA concluded fiscal year 2025 with $43,200 million ($43.20 billion) in liquidity, up from $25,980 million at the end of fiscal 2024.
- **Inventory:** Stood at $8,420 million ($8.42 billion), positioned to satisfy fulfillment cycles for Hopper platforms and the commercial ramp of Blackwell GB200 systems.
- **Total Debt:** $8,460 million ($8.46 billion), maintaining an investment-grade, net-cash position of $34.74 billion.

## 4. Capital Allocation and Shareholder Returns

NVIDIA maintained an aggressive capital return strategy during fiscal 2025:
- **Share Repurchases:** Repurchased 254 million shares of common stock for an aggregate value of $30,850 million ($30.85 billion).
- **Cash Dividends:** Paid $550 million in quarterly cash dividends to shareholders.
- **Total Capital Returned:** $31,400 million ($31.40 billion), representing 81.7% of annual free cash flow.
- **Remaining Authorization:** The Board of Directors authorized an additional $25.0 billion in share repurchases in August 2024, leaving approximately $22.2 billion in remaining authorization as of January 26, 2025.

## 5. First Quarter Fiscal 2026 Outlook

NVIDIA's guidance for the first quarter of fiscal year 2026 provided the following operational targets:
- **Revenue:** Expected to be $38.0 billion, plus or minus 2%.
- **Gross Margins:** GAAP gross margin expected to be 71.0% (±50 bps); Non-GAAP gross margin expected to be 71.5% (±50 bps). The sequential margin moderation reflects the introductory manufacturing ramp and packaging integration of Blackwell products.
- **Operating Expenses:** GAAP operating expenses projected at approximately $4.8 billion; Non-GAAP operating expenses projected at $3.6 billion.
- **Tax Rate:** GAAP and non-GAAP tax rates projected at 17.0%, plus or minus 1%.
