# NVIDIA Reports Record Fourth Quarter and Fiscal 2025 Financial Results
**Document Type:** Official Press Release  
**Issuer:** NVIDIA Corporation Investor Relations  
**Date of Release:** February 26, 2025  
**Location:** Santa Clara, California  
**Press Contact:** Ken Brown, NVIDIA Corporate Communications  
**Investor Relations:** Simona Jankowski, VP Investor Relations  

---

### SANTA CLARA, Calif. - Feb. 26, 2025 - 

NVIDIA today reported record revenue for the fourth quarter ended January 26, 2025, of $37.52 billion, up 7% sequentially from the third quarter and up 94% from $19.31 billion a year ago.

For the full fiscal year 2025, total revenue was $96.31 billion, up 122% from $43.48 billion a year earlier.

GAAP diluted earnings per share for the fourth quarter were $0.81, up 84% from $0.44 a year ago. Non-GAAP diluted earnings per share were $0.85, up 81% from $0.47 a year ago.

For the full fiscal year, GAAP diluted earnings per share were $2.14, up 85% from $1.19 in fiscal 2024. Non-GAAP diluted earnings per share were $2.23, up 87% from $1.19 a year ago.

### Key Financial Highlights and Market Platform Performance:

- **Data Center Platform:** Fourth-quarter revenue rose to a record $33.64 billion, up 112% from a year ago. Growth was powered by the deployment of the Hopper GPU platform and initial volume shipments of Blackwell systems. Full-year Data Center revenue soared 142% to $87.82 billion.
- **Gaming Platform:** Fourth-quarter revenue was $3.08 billion, up 9% from a year ago. Full-year revenue was $11.45 billion, up 10% from the prior year, driven by strong seasonal demand for GeForce RTX GPUs and AI-capable laptops.
- **Professional Visualization:** Fourth-quarter revenue reached $492 million, up 17% year-over-year. Full-year revenue grew 19% to $1.84 billion, led by enterprise adoption of RTX Ada workstations.
- **Automotive and Robotics:** Fourth-quarter revenue reached a record $512 million, surging 82% from a year ago. Full-year revenue expanded 57% to $1.71 billion, driven by the commercial adoption of NVIDIA DRIVE Orin by global EV manufacturers.

### Capital Return and Quarterly Dividend Declaration

During fiscal 2025, NVIDIA returned $31.40 billion to shareholders in the form of $30.85 billion in share repurchases and $550 million in cash dividends.

NVIDIA announced that its Board of Directors declared a quarterly cash dividend of $0.01 per share to be paid on April 3, 2025, to all stockholders of record on March 12, 2025.

In August 2024, the Board approved an additional $25.0 billion in share repurchase authorization without expiration, of which $22.2 billion remained available as of the end of fiscal 2025.

### Business Outlook for First Quarter Fiscal 2026:

- Revenue is expected to be $38.0 billion, plus or minus 2%.
- GAAP and non-GAAP gross margins are expected to be 71.0% and 71.5%, respectively, plus or minus 50 basis points.
- GAAP and non-GAAP operating expenses are expected to be approximately $4.8 billion and $3.6 billion, respectively.
- GAAP and non-GAAP tax rates are expected to be 17.0%, plus or minus 1%.
