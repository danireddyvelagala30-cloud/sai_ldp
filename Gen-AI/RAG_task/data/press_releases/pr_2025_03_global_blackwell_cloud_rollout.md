# NVIDIA and Cloud Leaders Accelerate Worldwide Rollout of Blackwell Supercomputing Clusters
**Document Type:** Official Press Release  
**Issuer:** NVIDIA Corporation Global Enterprise Alliances  
**Date of Release:** March 18, 2025  
**Location:** San Jose, California (GTC 2025)  
**Press Contact:** Liz Vattimo, NVIDIA Cloud Communications  

---

### SAN JOSE, Calif. - March 18, 2025 - 

NVIDIA today announced that the world’s leading cloud service providers—including Amazon Web Services (AWS), Google Cloud, Microsoft Azure, and Oracle Cloud Infrastructure (OCI)—are actively deploying tens of thousands of liquid-cooled NVIDIA GB200 NVL72 rack-scale systems to meet staggering global demand for generative AI training and multi-trillion-parameter inference.

This coordinated infrastructure rollout represents the largest hardware transition in enterprise cloud computing history, establishing modern "AI Factories" across North America, Europe, Japan, and the Asia-Pacific region.

---

### Cloud Titan Deployment Milestones:

- **Microsoft Azure:** Unveiled its Azure ND GB200 v6 VM series, interconnecting 72 Blackwell GPUs per rack over 1.8 TB/s NVLink. Azure announced that OpenAI will utilize Blackwell superclusters for next-generation reasoning frontier models.
- **Amazon Web Services (AWS):** Began volume deployment of Ceiba—the monumental supercomputer jointly engineered with NVIDIA featuring 20,736 Blackwell GPUs and 10,368 Grace CPUs interconnected over AWS Nitro and EFA (Elastic Fabric Adapter) networking.
- **Google Cloud:** Integrated GB200 NVL72 instances into Google Cloud Vertex AI, offering enterprise customers up to 30x faster inference on Google Gemma 2 and open multimodal foundation models.
- **Oracle Cloud Infrastructure (OCI):** Selected NVIDIA GB200 systems to power its OCI Supercluster, enabling sovereign AI clouds and enterprise-grade multi-cloud deployments with native liquid-cooled data center architectures.

---

### Sovereign AI Momentum Across Nations

In addition to commercial hyperscalers, NVIDIA announced landmark Sovereign AI partnerships:
- **Japan:** Japanese telecommunications giant SoftBank Corp. is building Japan’s most powerful sovereign AI supercomputer utilizing NVIDIA DGX B200 systems to accelerate national language models and cellular telecom generative AI services.
- **France & Europe:** Scaleway and Mistral AI are expanding sovereign European AI clusters powered by Blackwell and NVIDIA Quantum-2 InfiniBand.
- **Singapore & Canada:** National research institutes deployed Blackwell infrastructure to power sovereign genomics, quantum simulation, and climate research initiatives.

---

### Networking Backbone: 800 Gb/s Quantum-X and Spectrum-X

To connect hundreds of thousands of Blackwell GPUs across distributed data centers, cloud providers have standardized on NVIDIA networking:
- **NVIDIA Quantum-X800 InfiniBand:** Delivers 800 Gb/s throughput per port with ultra-low latency, purpose-engineered for multi-month foundation model training runs.
- **NVIDIA Spectrum-X800 Ethernet:** Purpose-built for multi-tenant generative AI cloud architectures, delivering 1.6x higher network performance compared to traditional Ethernet switches.

> "The data center is the new unit of computing," said Jensen Huang, founder and CEO of NVIDIA. "With Blackwell, cloud providers and sovereign nations are deploying true AI factories that transform electricity into intelligence."
