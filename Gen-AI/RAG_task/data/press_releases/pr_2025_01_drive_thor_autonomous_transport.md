# NVIDIA DRIVE Thor Centralized Superchip Selected by Premier Global Automakers and Autonomous Fleets
**Document Type:** Official Press Release  
**Issuer:** NVIDIA Corporation Automotive Business Unit  
**Date of Release:** January 07, 2025  
**Location:** Las Vegas, Nevada (CES 2025)  
**Press Contact:** Marie Cabret, NVIDIA Automotive Public Relations  

---

### LAS VEGAS - Jan. 07, 2025 - 

NVIDIA today announced widespread automotive and mobility industry adoption of the NVIDIA DRIVE™ Thor centralized autonomous car computer. 

Successor to the widely deployed DRIVE Orin processor, DRIVE Thor integrates next-generation NVIDIA Blackwell architecture graphics and Tensor Cores alongside high-performance CPU cores to deliver 2,000 teraflops (TFLOPS) of 4-bit floating-point AI compute. This unmatched processing density allows automakers to consolidate historically isolated distributed Electronic Control Units (ECUs)—including automated driving, active safety, surround vision, automated parking, driver monitoring, and in-vehicle generative AI digital assistants—into a single centralized supercomputer.

---

### Leading Global EV Automakers Commit to DRIVE Thor:

- **BYD:** The world’s largest electric vehicle manufacturer is expanding its long-standing collaboration with NVIDIA to integrate DRIVE Thor across its premium Dynasty, Ocean, and Yangwang vehicle models, enabling end-to-end automated navigation from factory to highway.
- **Li Auto:** The premier Chinese luxury intelligent electric vehicle maker selected DRIVE Thor to power its next-generation commercial autonomous driving computing system, Li AD Max.
- **Zeekr & Geely Group:** Premium EV manufacturer Zeekr will launch its luxury EV models powered by DRIVE Thor, utilizing centralized compute for both autonomous highway pilot and high-definition generative infotainment.
- **XPENG & Hyper (GAC):** Integrating DRIVE Thor to accelerate level 3 highway and urban autonomous driving with real-time world-model vision transformers.

---

### Centralized Brain for Autonomous Trucking and Robotaxi Fleets:

Beyond passenger vehicles, autonomous commercial transport leaders have standardized on DRIVE Thor:
- **Plus (Autonomous Trucking):** Selected DRIVE Thor to power its SuperDrive automated trucking system, executing dense perception algorithms to ensure commercial heavy-duty freight safety.
- **Waabi:** Integrating DRIVE Thor into its generative AI-first autonomous trucking platform, combining foundation world models with real-time vehicle actuation.
- **Nuro:** Selected DRIVE Thor to power its fourth-generation autonomous delivery robot, handling urban delivery across residential neighborhoods.

---

### Safety and Software Architecture

DRIVE Thor is designed from the silicon up to satisfy the highest automotive functional safety standards:
- **Functional Safety:** Certified for ISO 26262 ASIL D safety compliance.
- **Hardware Isolation:** Incorporates advanced hardware isolation features that allow critical autonomous driving tasks and non-critical cockpit infotainment applications to execute simultaneously without cross-talk or performance preemption.
- **NVIDIA DriveOS & DRIVE AGX:** Supported by NVIDIA DriveOS, providing a secure, real-time operating foundation certified for multi-camera surround perception and radar/LiDAR fusion.

> "Accelerated computing and generative AI are transforming the mobility industry," said Danny Shapiro, Vice President of Automotive at NVIDIA. "DRIVE Thor provides automakers with an all-in-one AI supercomputer that slashes vehicle harness weight, reduces manufacturing costs, and enables continuously upgraded autonomous driving capabilities over the life of the vehicle."
